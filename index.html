Subscription & Cancellation

Plans

  • Free: up to 3 draft dossiers, max 3 new dossiers per 7 days.
  • Standard: up to 30 draft dossiers, unlimited new dossiers, PDF/ZIP export.
  • Pro: unlimited drafts, PDF/ZIP export, batch exports, priority support.

Billing & renewals (Apple Media Services): subscriptions are charged to your Apple ID, renew automatically, and can be cancelled at least 24 hours before renewal. Manage from your Apple ID > Subscriptions or directly via the Apple link.
